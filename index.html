Master Fictional Vignette: Sofia

"Sofia is a high-achieving student who sets extremely ambitious goals. She feels intense anxiety whenever her grades drop below 95%, works late into the night, seeks constant reassurance from teachers, and observed her mother manage stress through rigorous organization."

1. Psychodynamic Lens (Freud)

Explains behavior through an overly harsh Superego instilling unconscious guilt and defense mechanisms (reaction formation/perfectionism) to manage anxiety.

Key Focus: Unconscious anxiety & rigid Superego standards.
2. Trait Model Lens (Big Five OCEAN)

Describes Sofia's position on continuous dimensions: High Conscientiousness (ambitious, dutiful) paired with High Neuroticism (anxious about grade drops).

Key Focus: Stable dimensional trait scores (High C, High N).
3. Humanistic Lens (Rogers / Maslow)

Identifies incongruence between Sofia's Real Self and Ideal Self, driven by conditional worth (believing acceptance requires top academic scores).

Key Focus: Incongruence & desire for Unconditional Positive Regard.
4. Social-Cognitive Lens (Bandura)

Highlights observational learning (modeling her mother's organizational habits) and reciprocal determinism (teacher praise reinforcing reassurance-seeking behavior).

Key Focus: Modeling, teacher reinforcement & outcome expectations.
